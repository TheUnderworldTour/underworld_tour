# Requirements

## Problem Statement
Many players who enjoy rhythm and roguelike games have limited opportunities to find both categories apply to one title. We aim to create an experience that marries the 2 genres into a fun and streamlined software that will boast the replayability of roguelike games, and the punchy gameplay of rhythm games.
